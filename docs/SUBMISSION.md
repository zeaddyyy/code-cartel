# Submission readiness

NetraX uses Model 1 (registry and GIS foundation) as the common asset layer and extends it with unified viewing, AI analytics, events, and a regional scale architecture. The own-feed proof is the YOLO/ByteTrack → Express → PostgreSQL flow. Government feeds and VAHAN, SARTHI, eGujCop, AFIS, and NAFIS are protected adapter boundaries requiring authorized access; no government data is fabricated.
